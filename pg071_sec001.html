Vocabulary

Sign

a symbol that represents something or shows
the identity of a certain thing or event

Dangerous

objects, conditions or environment that can
cause pain, injury or death

Clothes

garments that are worn by humans to cover
the body or part of the body

Environment

all things that surround humans

Cleanliness

the condition of keeping the environment free
from dirt, waste and trash

Safety

the state of being free from or being protected
from danger, harm, risk or injury
