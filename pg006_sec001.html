Introduction

This book has been prepared based on curriculum and syllabus of Primary Education Standards I and II of the year 2023 issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. Moreover, the book is the result of improvement and the combination of the two Health and Environment books for Standards One and Two published in the year 2018 based on the 2016 curriculum and syllabus. The objective of this book is to enable the pupils to build competencies in taking care of health and the environment through the following learning activities:

1. Cleaning the body;

2. Taking care of clothes;

3. Protection from dangerous environment and objects; and 4. Identifying safety signs.

Additional learning resources are available at TIE e-Library https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
