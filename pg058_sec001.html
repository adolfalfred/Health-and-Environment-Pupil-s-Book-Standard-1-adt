Questions

Read or listen to the story below, then answer the questions that follow.

Story

One day Neema and her mother went to the farm to harvest maize. While her mother was harvesting, Neema was playing under a big tree. While playing, Neema was stung by a bee which was on a flower of the tree, and she felt severe pain. Neema's face was swollen, so her mother took her to hospital. The doctor gave Neema medicine and told her that bees are dangerous insects that sting and leave poison in the body, causing various effects. The doctor also told Neema that other dangerous insects are ants, mosquitoes and wasps. The doctor advised Neema to avoid dangerous insects.

Questions

1. What have you learnt from the story?

2. Mention dangerous insects from the story.

3. Why did the doctor advise Neema to avoid dangerous
insects?

Activity 1

(a) Listen to the video and follow the audio description or watch videos on the internet showing dangerous insects.

(b) Explore the school compound safely with a teacher or classmate. Identify dangerous insects through observation or an accessible description, then write their names.
