Questions

Read the story below and answer the questions that follow.

Story

One day Neema and her mother went to the farm to

harvest maize. While her mother was harvesting, Neema
was playing under a big tree. While playing, Neema was

stung by a bee which was on a flower of the tree and she

felt severe pain. Neema’s face was swollen, so her mother

took her to hospital. The doctor gave Neema medicine

and told her that bees are dangerous insects that sting

and leave poison in the body causing various effects. The

doctor also told Neema that other dangerous insects are

ants, mosquitoes and wasps. The doctor advised Neema
to avoid dangerous insects.

Questions

1. What have you learnt from the story?

2. Mention dangerous insects from the story.

3. Why did the doctor advise Neema to avoid dangerous
insects?

Activity 1

(a) Watch video on the internet showing pictures of
dangerous insects.

(b) Walk around the school compound, and look for dangerous
insects, then write their names.
